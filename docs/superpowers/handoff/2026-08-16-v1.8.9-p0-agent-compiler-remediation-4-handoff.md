## Remediation completed in this line

- Replaced compiler-side proof globals and caller-reachable snapshot bootstrap
  with a host-issued, socket-backed `HostAuthorityHandleV1`. The compiler,
  discovery receipt and capability selection all require the live host handle;
  scope, principal, session and project bindings are resolved and revalidated
  by the host channel rather than reconstructed from caller JSON.
- Closed the old `_issue` and `_from_server_snapshots` issuance seams. Direct
  constructors, `object.__new__`, imported tokens, copied proof objects and
  pickle/JSON round trips cannot become accepted compiler authority. Malformed
  selection proofs map to the stable typed `FORGED_AUTHORITY` failure.
- Enforced the natural-language allowlist on every public and internal
  compilation route. A disabled capability cannot be smuggled through a
  direct typed request or the registry QA seam.
- Bound durable compilation reservations to an admitted non-symlink FD and
  `(device, inode)` identity. Replacing the root pathname cannot create a
  second owner; stores without durable authority fail closed before reserve.
- Kept the locked C0 `ServerCompilationRecordIssuerV1` untouched and explicit
  as an open contract/cutover dependency.
