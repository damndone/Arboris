## Defect and resolution

At the remediation baseline, `_parquet_engine()` admitted `fastparquet` while
`read_frame_bounded()` directly imported only pyarrow. Fastparquet-only could
therefore appear available to planning and fail during bounded execution.

The implementation now has one fail-closed admission boundary. `_parquet_engine`
admits pyarrow only because its batch iterator is the verified bounded reader;
fastparquet-only and neither are consistently typed unavailable for capability,
writer/read-back, and bounded ingestion. A future fastparquet adapter requires
its own verified row-cap, filter, and boundary contract before admission.
