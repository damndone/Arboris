## Scope and production contract

Only `backend/workbench/agent/context_tools.py`, its owned tests, and the
remediation-4 objective/plan were changed in the product commit. The Survey
statistical engine, weights, falsey declarations, exposure, FPC, Survey Report
visibility, Notebook receipt boundary, and central consumer code were not
changed.

The projection contract now does the following:

- Coefficient terms are admitted only when they are non-empty, bounded strings
  of at most 128 characters. Empty, whitespace-only, overlong, non-string, and
  empty nested coefficient cells are omitted. Valid cells retain only bounded
  typed evidence; invalid declared cells retain a typed unavailable reason and
  never receive `None`, `[]`, or empty nested placeholders.
- Artifact numeric fields use field semantics: probabilities are `[0, 1]`,
  row counts are bounded non-negative integers, standard errors are positive,
  non-negative fields reject negative values, and confidence intervals require
  both finite endpoints in lower-to-upper order. Invalid fields are omitted
  from Agent/Notebook artifact packets and receive
  `projection_status=unavailable` with
  `unavailable_reason=AGENT_ARTIFACT_NUMERIC_INVALID`.
- P7 nested result projection is capped at depth 32. At the cap and beyond,
  projection returns exactly typed unavailable evidence with
  `unavailable_reason=AGENT_ARTIFACT_NESTING_TOO_DEEP`; nested raw content is
  not returned and no bare `RecursionError` is raised.
- Notebook artifact receipts remain a closed typed allowlist. Unknown optional
  fields and raw rows/payloads are dropped; only declared provenance, design,
  replicate, coefficient, inference, and bounded numeric evidence can cross
  into the packet/LLM context.
