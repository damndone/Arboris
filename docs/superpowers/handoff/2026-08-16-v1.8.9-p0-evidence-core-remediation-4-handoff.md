## Implemented controls

- `EvidenceRegistryV1` direct construction is rejected. The explicit
  `create_server_evidence_registry` factory issues an authority proof bound to
  the registry object identity and scope. `object.__new__` and copied registry
  fields fail closed before admission or durable reads.
- Registry persistence receipts remain process-local capabilities and now have
  stable malformed-proof handling. Receipt validation includes the owning
  registry, exact admitted record, nonce, seal, and complete artifact hash.
- `EvidencePersistenceAdmissionV1` is bound to a server-issued proof, root
  device/inode, owner PID, and admission object identity. Field-copied or
  cross-process tokens cannot construct a writer or write evidence.
- Persistence `load` validates registry authority and receipt capability before
  reading commit, packet, or index files. Invalid proof objects produce the
  typed `EVIDENCE_PERSISTENCE_ADMISSION_REQUIRED` failure.
- Projection views and outcomes require a server-only HMAC proof. Recomputed
  payload digests, copied proof fields, `object.__new__` outcomes, and mutated
  views fail during construction or serialization. Nested projections remain
  recursively read-only.
