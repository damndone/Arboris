## What advanced beyond v1.8.8

This candidate adds a typed and evidence-bound foundation around the existing
statistical capabilities. In practical terms:

- Agent and Notebook confirmation paths are bound to the server-owned compiler,
  admission, provider dispatch and persisted identity boundary; this is a
  shared semantic foundation, not a second Notebook engine.
- P0 workflow truth and resume hardening persist and rebind completed model
  branches, artifacts, output bindings and lineage before callbacks or writes.
- Report and export consumers read owner-bound, run-bound, artifact-bound,
  SHA-256-verified typed evidence and fail closed for foreign, corrupt,
  missing or unsupported evidence instead of recomputing or showing raw JSON.
- The data-consumer layer adds typed metadata/profile/missing/filter/sort/split/
  recode and preview-confirm-child-lineage contracts, with bounded interchange
  readers/writers and explicit unavailable states for unsupported native files.
- Classification ML adds task-aware splitting/CV, leakage and entity/temporal
  boundaries, holdout/evidence status, threshold/calibration and bounded
  explainability projections consumed by Agent/Notebook/Report surfaces.
- Survey extensions use the existing variance engine and family adapters, keep
  unsupported designs fail-closed, and prevent naive model output from being
  labelled as survey output.
- Provider catalog and coverage are deterministic and identity-bound; blocked
  projection rows remain visible instead of being counted as capabilities.
