## Delivered

- `ProviderContributionV1` now carries immutable provider-owned consumer
  projection declarations. The current wire format requires the declaration
  field; legacy or incomplete payloads do not silently enter the current
  runtime.
- Built-in manifests declare consumer identity, evidence/envelope contract
  references, fact kinds, policy, renderer, budgets, sensitivity, ordering,
  and coverage status. Coverage uses declarations as its source of truth and
  preserves missing versus explicitly exempt rows.
- Central projection accepts only an exact catalog loaded and issued by the
  server-owned catalog loader. Reconstructed or caller-forged catalog objects
  fail closed. The authenticity digest includes the closure-owned resolver
  contract-ref state, so replacing it with an empty resolver is also rejected.
- Central policy ceilings constrain sensitivity, token budget, row budget, and
  ordering even when a provider declaration is changed.
- Projection admission verifies accepted evidence-contract identity,
  accepted envelope identity, and declared fact kinds against persisted typed
  evidence. Unknown or undeclared facts are not projected.
- The dynamic data-consumer provider builder and the contract-lock fixtures
  emit the same current declaration wire shape; existing catalog consumers
  were rerun after the wire change.
- Core consumer declarations are wired to the shared Agent/Notebook/Report
  projection path. Package-specific declarations remain explicit catalog rows
  but are blocked until their producers expose matching persisted identities;
  this avoids claiming unsupported central coverage.

## Safety interpretation

The catalog is now extensible without making consumer defaults the authority,
but declarations are not unrestricted code execution. The server-owned loader,
central ceilings, evidence identity checks, and explicit blocked coverage are
all required before a future provider or model family becomes consumable.
