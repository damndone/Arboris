## Implemented controls

- `ArtifactAdmissionV1` remains a public value contract but is no longer a
  persistence authority. `EvidenceRegistryV1.persistence_receipt()` issues a
  process-scoped, registry-owned sealed receipt; `EvidencePacketStoreV1.commit`
  requires that exact receipt and rejects a direct record, a receipt from a
  different registry, and a forged token before any write.
- Artifact `content_hash` now covers artifact id/type/scope, source,
  validator, execution, contract digest, and the canonical envelope. Packet
  and index rows are checked against the complete reconstructed row on both
  `load` and `load_packet`.
- Source and provenance values entering an admission are closed opaque refs or
  validated contract refs. Slash, backslash, URL, relative/path-like, and raw
  `original_path` provenance are rejected before persistence, including
  path-like `ContractRefV1` validator identities.
- Packet and index containers require exact fields plus exact
  `contract_version` and `producer_version`; forged versions and metadata
  bindings fail closed with stable persistence errors.
- Invalid projection ids are sanitized before failure receipt construction.
  Nested mappings and sequences in successful projections are recursively
  frozen; unavailable, blocked, corrupt, partial, and insufficient evidence do
  not become successful projections.
