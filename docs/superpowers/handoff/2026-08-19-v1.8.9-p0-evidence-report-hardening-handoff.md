## Outcome

The Report consumer now has a fail-closed typed evidence boundary. An indexed
post-estimation artifact without an explicitly parseable committed
`EvidenceCommitBindingV1` and matching `EvidenceEnvelopeV1` produces a typed
`UNAVAILABLE` projection with a reason code. A malformed or digest-mismatched
canonical pair produces a typed `BLOCKED` projection. Neither path copies raw
result fields or recursively extracts scalar leaves.

The run detail route exposes `report_evidence`. `ReportWorkspaceContext` parses
that server-owned projection, builds citable post-estimation facts only from
its bounded typed facts when status is `available`, and passes the projection
through the real `ReportView` to `FamilyEvidence`. The renderer shows status,
envelope digest, commit/provenance and bounded facts. The old model-result and
artifact props remain available for legacy direct callers, but the real
ReportView path does not pass or render them as FamilyEvidence.
