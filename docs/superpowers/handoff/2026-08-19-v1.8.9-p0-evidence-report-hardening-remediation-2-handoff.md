## Outcome

The independent-spec remediation is complete at the package-local evidence and
Report boundary. The importable `_REPORT_FACTORY_TOKEN` seam was removed.
Projection, XLSX, HTML, and PDF writer paths now require the real C0
`ServerOwnedReportEvidencePersistenceV1` owner marker and recheck the complete
canonical server wire before render or filesystem IO. Caller-created tokens,
forged owners, forged/serialized projection objects, synchronized forged facts
and digest, raw table rows, foreign roots, and unowned XLSX render calls fail
closed.

projection digest. Digest, identity, and provenance mismatches remain typed
unavailable. `ReportView` labels prediction research compatibility evidence as
NON-CITABLE display-only; citable facts continue to come only from the typed
server-owned projection. Legacy model results, post-estimation payloads, and
raw artifact JSON do not enter the writer facts.

The append writer now generates a UTC millisecond timestamp while holding the
append lock, strictly after the current tail when the clock collides. The
existing append-only hash-chain, tail anchor, and strict verifier remain
unchanged; caller event objects are not mutated.
