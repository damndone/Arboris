## Delivered

### Survey evidence

- Added a server-owned `workbench.survey.report` provider projection and bound
  it to the Survey provider catalog, evidence contract, policy, and renderer.
- Agent, Notebook, and Report use the same Survey projection adapter and typed
  unavailable reasons for missing, unsupported, malformed, or degraded source
  evidence.
- Run detail now exposes a typed Survey evidence envelope with server-owned
  authority and a receipt bound to the artifact, source receipt, catalog digest,
  and projection digest.
- Survey estimation persists an explicit validated or degraded evidence status;
  missing status is not silently treated as success.

### Data-consumer and Report evidence

- Report reads the server-owned data-consumer projection rather than raw
  operation artifacts or recomputing facts.
- The Report data renderer validates fact identity, source lineage, catalog
  digest, evidence digest, projection digest, and receipt identity before
  rendering.
- The Report Survey renderer validates model identity, artifact/source hashes,
  catalog references, design-variance evidence, projection digest, and receipt
  identity before rendering.
- Invalid, missing, stale, forged, or unsupported evidence is shown as typed
  unavailable/degraded state; raw JSON and raw paths are not rendered.
