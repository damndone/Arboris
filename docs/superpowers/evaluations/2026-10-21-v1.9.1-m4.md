## Delivered

  provenance/version, stale-review rejection and per-entry disable switches.
  Main and chain discovery descriptors contain no project-authored instructions.
- `external.query` is declared once in the shared workflow/operation registry.
  Fixed HTTPS GET/JSON queries require ordinary human operation approval in both
  modes plus separate egress consent; outputs are source-recorded untrusted data.
  The endpoint version is declared metadata, not an independently authenticated
  remote software version. No arbitrary executable or remote-write transport.
- Four production hook slots: before/after operations, turn end, plan approval.
  The common script-export boundary is also covered under `script.export`.
  Hooks may record or deny, never grant authority. Receipts are atomic/idempotent;
  exceptional failures receive an after receipt without changing effect truth.
  The follow-up below extends file-export coverage to the shared service and delivery boundaries.
- Usage UI now exposes total/cache miss/reasoning, request/event identity and
  original usage. Inconsistencies stay unknown; reasoning is not counted twice.
  Cost remains `pricing_unverified`, not an asserted invoice.
