# Panel and DID analysis

Establish whether the question is a conditional panel association or an intervention effect. Fix outcome scale, entity/time identifiers, treatment timing, target population and estimand in the plan. Inspect the original groups, duplicates, missingness and unbalanced periods without inventing or filling observations.

## Analysis and evidence

1. Fit the declared one-way/two-way fixed effects or supported DID through the common operation and approval path. Interpret the actual effect absorbed and total parameter rank. Verify the retained sample and actual source, not only a configuration summary.
2. Read native within-residual/rank/group evidence. Consecutive observed rows do not prove a one-period lag. Dependence within an entity requires appropriate inference; row-wise HC does not certify fixed-T FE. Fewer than 10 clusters remains unverified under the current completion rule; do not relabel it as adequate independence.
3. For DID, inspect treatment cohorts, control eligibility and estimable pre-period leads. Missing or unidentified leads are unavailable, not a passed parallel-trends test. Rejected pretrends require explicit design revision. Non-rejection does not identify the treatment effect.
4. Generic staggered TWFE coefficients are not automatically an ATT. Preserve native interpretation restrictions; an applicable alternative needs an explicitly stated estimand and supported estimator, never a silent method substitution.
5. Obtain actual, justified sensitivity fits with source/sample/target differences recorded. If the interpretation or diagnostic gap cannot be resolved, complete independent approved work and retain the affected gap. Do not seek a specification with a favorable p-value.

## Completion and report

Use panel/did completion playbooks and verified references in update_plan. Inspect current native completion evidence, including model-bound diagnostic companions. The report distinguishes association/causality, actual covariance and cluster count, pretrend limitations and any exploratory revisions. A frozen-workflow specification curve must retain failed/unattempted members and cannot merge different targets/scales as equivalent estimates. Requirements met is not inferential or causal certification. Keep both failed attempts; pause after the second. Isolated review grants no permission and cannot stand in for an external statistical oracle.
