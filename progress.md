## 2026-08-08 — Stage 18 final extension batch and freeze

- Implemented six standalone P7 packs across five extension areas: nonparametric/robust inference, GLM extensions, model diagnostics, global spatial statistics, and Matching/Synthetic Control. Each pack has a closed typed contract, stable operation IDs, bounded JSON-safe output, explicit status/reason semantics, scope metadata, adversarial tests, and an external numerical Oracle.
- Nonparametric operations are rank-based tests plus descriptive robust summaries. Exact/asymptotic/permutation and missing/tie/zero policies are explicit; the pack does not silently turn rank evidence into mean evidence or an omnibus result into pairwise claims.
- GLM operations are ZIP, ZINB, hurdle Poisson, hurdle negative binomial, and Beta regression. Zero, count, positive, mean, and precision estimands are kept separate. Hurdle negative-binomial positive-count standard errors are explicitly approximate BFGS-inverse/normal-approximation evidence, not a general sandwich guarantee.
- Model diagnostics are OLS-shaped, typed-request-only evidence: VIF, Breusch–Pagan, White, Breusch–Godfrey, RESET, and influence. A diagnostic p-value never becomes a `model_valid` verdict; clustered/survey/nonlinear covariance semantics are outside this pack.
- Spatial operations are global Moran's I, Geary's C, and Getis–Ord G with explicit weights/graph policies and seeded permutation inference. Spatial lag/error regression, local hotspot statistics, and learned adjacency remain deferred.
- Matching reports ATT/balance evidence under explicit assumptions. The v1 contract names logit propensity support distance, while the current nearest-neighbour geometry is deliberately reported as `standardized_covariate_euclidean_v1`; true propensity-distance matching and typed trimming semantics are future work and must not be inferred from this release.
- Synthetic Control reports constrained donor weights, pre-fit quality, post-treatment gaps, and bounded placebo summaries. It does not emit placebo significance p-values, does not accept predictor matrices in v1, and does not implement augmented/regularized/automatic-donor variants.
- External validation: base-R/direct-formula Oracles passed for nonparametric, spatial, Matching, and Synthetic Control; statsmodels Oracles passed for GLM and diagnostics.
